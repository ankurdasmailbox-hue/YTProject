2. 2026 ALGORITHM LEVERS & RETENTION ENGINEERING
================================================================================
The algorithm optimizes for Cumulative Session Watch Time and Satisfied View Duration.

Core Algorithmic Levers:
1. The 5-Second Sensory Pattern Interrupt (Sammy's Rule):
   - Zero slow logo animations or channel introductions.
   - Start immediately inside the cataclysm or mystery (e.g., magma ocean dissolving,
     meteor impact mid-strike, pressure dial surging to 200 atm).
2. The 45-Second Curiosity Loop Engine:
   - Introduce a scientific paradox or tension every 45 seconds before resolving
     the previous one.
   - Pacing target: 125-135 Words Per Minute (WPM) with continuous camera kinetic motion.
3. Clean Video Mastering (Soft SRT Delivery):
   - Master video stream contains ZERO burned-in subtitles.
   - Captions delivered via soft English SRT (YouTube player CC toggle).
   - Keeps full visual clarity for high-resolution 1080p/1440p playback.
4. The Shorts-to-Long-Form Conversion Flywheel:
   - Every long-form episode yields 2-3 viral 9:16 vertical Shorts.
   - Bottom CTA banner: "FULL STORY LINKED IN CHANNEL" drives top-of-funnel mobile
     viewers directly into long-form episodes for watch-hour accumulation.

3. VIDEO FORMATTING, METADATA & PACKAGING RULES
================================================================================
A. Master Documentaries:
   - Aspect Ratio: 16:9 (1920x1080 minimum, mastered to 2560x1440 QHD for VP9/AV1 codec).
   - Framerate: 30 fps or 60 fps.
   - Ideal Runtime: 8:00 – 12:30 minutes (qualifies for manual mid-roll ad placement).
   - Audio: -14 LUFS loudness normalization with mild background ambient score ducked at -20 dB.

B. YouTube Shorts:
   - Aspect Ratio: 9:16 Vertical (1080x1920).
   - Duration: 32 – 42 seconds (sweet spot for >100% completion rate).
   - Canvas: Ambient blurred motion background + centered crisp 16:9 hero stage.
   - Top Banner: High-contrast yellow/white uppercase text bar.

C. Packaging & A/B Thumbnails:
   - Every episode generates 3 distinct tested thumbnail candidates (A, B, C).
   - Formula: One dominant illustrated subject + 3-4 bold impact words max + Era Badge.
   - Titles: Avoid generic openers like "The History of...".
     Use: [Specific Event/Cataclysm] — [What Actually Happened / High Stakes]
     Example: "When a Mars-Sized Planet Smashed into Earth at 25,000 MPH"

D. Dual-Layer Hashtag Architecture (Relevant + Current Trending):
   YouTube's search, browse features, and Shorts shelf prioritize content through a combination of high-relevance semantic tags and broad trending engagement velocity.
   We deploy a dual-layer hashtag strategy pairing core evergreen authority with high-velocity trending reach:

   1. Tier 1: Core Evergreen / High-RPM Relevant Hashtags
      - Purpose: High semantic indexing, deep-time niche authority, and Category 27 advertiser bid matching ($7.00 - $14.50 RPM).
      - Core Relevant Tags:
        #EarthHistory #PlanetaryScience #DeepTime #GeologyDocumentary #SpaceDocumentary #Science #AncientEarth #Astrobiology #OriginOfLife

   2. Tier 2: Current Trending & Broad Discovery Hashtags
      - Purpose: Tapping into YouTube's broad recommendation algorithms, Trending feeds, and Shorts viral discovery loops for wider audience reach.
      - Current Trending / Broad Virality Tags:
        #Trending #Viral #LearnOnYouTube #ScienceFacts #DidYouKnow #SpaceFacts #Documentary #Shorts #MindBlown #EducationalContent

   3. Placement & Formatting Rules:
      - Long-Form Master Episodes:
        * Volume: 5 to 7 hashtags total in description body (YouTube prominently displays the top 3 above video title or top of description).
        * Allocation Formula: 3-4 Core Relevant Niche Tags + 2 Current Trending / Broad Educational Discovery Tags.
        * Standard Bundle: #EarthHistory #PlanetaryScience #GeologyDocumentary #Trending #LearnOnYouTube #ScienceFacts
        * Policy Guardrail: Never exceed 15 hashtags (YouTube will ignore all tags and flag video as spam under Search & Discovery guidelines).
      - YouTube Shorts:
        * Placement: Direct inclusion in title (#Shorts) + 3-5 hashtags in description.
        * Allocation Formula: Combine #Shorts and high-velocity discovery tags (#Trending, #ScienceFacts) with 2 core topic tags (#EarthHistory, #PlanetaryScience) to capture both search and Shorts shelf traffic.

4. 15-DAY REVIEW CHECKLIST & PIVOT TRIGGERS
================================================================================
Check every 15 days automatically:
1. Click-Through Rate (CTR) Benchmark:
   - If CTR < 4.0% after 7 days -> Immediately swap thumbnail to Candidate B or C and
     test alternate Curiosity-Gap title formula.
2. Average Percentage Viewed (APV):
   - If APV < 35% on an 8-min video -> Tighten script pacing in Act 1 & 2; front-load
     stakes in the first 30 seconds.
3. Shorts Conversion Rate:
   - Track traffic source in Studio Analytics: If "Related video" clicks < 3%, revise
     Shorts bottom CTA banner wording to more aggressive curiosity hook.
4. Watch-Hour Run Rate:
   - Compare linear pace toward 4,000 hours against Jan 15, 2027 deadline.
   - Accelerate long-form publish cadence from 2 to 3 episodes/week if pace lags.
5. Hashtag & Discovery Velocity Audit:
   - Review YouTube Studio Traffic Sources -> "YouTube search" and "Suggested videos".
   - If search impressions stall, rotate Tier 2 trending tags based on YouTube Trends (Explore > Trending / Google Trends Science & Tech).
   - Ensure trending tags maintain topical alignment with planetary science to preserve viewer satisfaction and high APV.
